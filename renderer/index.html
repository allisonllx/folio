YOUR LOCAL LIBRARY

A small place for big possibilities.

Folio reads your local skill files. Your favorites, shelves, notes and run reviews stay on this Mac. Your original skills are never changed.

Opening Folio

Close the window to keep Folio in the menu bar. Use Folio → Quit to stop it. Launch at login is not enabled automatically.

Where we look

Plugin caches can include inactive copies. Finding a file does not confirm that your agent has enabled it. “Personal” means a personal skill folder, not necessarily a skill you authored.

Where your notes live

Shelves are suggested using keywords. Run reviews are your own records, not automatically captured sessions or quality scores.
